# Credits and sources

## Photos
All photos are loaded at runtime from **Wikimedia Commons** via the Wikipedia API. Only freely licensed images are shown. The author and licence appear on each photo; clicking the caption opens the original. The full list is in the app under **About → Photos and credits**.

Places without a Wikipedia article (e.g. Bernatka footbridge, Mariacka Street, Plac Nowy, Collegium Minus) show an illustration instead.
